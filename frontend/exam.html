Exam Instructions & Rules
Please read carefully before starting your exam
What counts as a violation
🔁
Tab switching — Do not switch to another browser tab or window during the exam. Each switch is recorded as a violation.
🖱️
Window focus loss — Do not click outside the exam window or minimize the browser.
📋
Copy & Paste — Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V, Ctrl+X and Ctrl+A are blocked. Using them will count as a violation.
⬅️
Back button — Pressing the browser back button during the exam is recorded as a violation.
🔧
Developer tools — Opening browser developer tools (F12) is blocked and counts as a violation.
🔒
3 violations = Exam locked — After 3 violations your exam is locked and an invigilator must unlock it. Your violation log will be visible to staff.
How the exam works
❓
One question at a time — Questions appear one by one. Use Save & Next to move forward and save your answer.
🟢
Green = Answered — Question has a saved answer.
🔴
Red = Seen but unanswered — You visited but did not answer.
🟣
Purple = Save & Review — Answered but marked to revisit.
💾
Auto-save — Your answers and timer are saved every 10 seconds. Refreshing the page will restore your progress.
By clicking I Understand, Start Exam you confirm that you have read and agree to follow these rules.
